Summary: MRX Naamkaran is a 100% Free and Ad-Free application. We do not show third-party ads, do not require account registration, do not collect sensitive personal data, do not sell user information, and do not process any payments or subscriptions.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MRX Naamkaran ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how MRX Naamkaran handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices (including Amazon Appstore, Uptodown, APKPure, and other distribution platforms).

2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used

MRX Naamkaran is designed to work without collecting personally identifiable information (PII).

  • No Account Required: You do not need to sign up, log in, or provide your name, phone number, email address, or location to use the app.
  • Name Generation Inputs: When you select filters such as Gender, Tradition/Dharm, Starting Letter, Rashi, Vibe/Style, Date/Time of Birth (for Lucky Rashi & Shubh Akshar calculation), or optional Parent/Sibling first names, these inputs are used solely to generate relevant baby name suggestions for you.
  • Local On-Device Storage: Your saved/favorite baby names, search history, family notes, and app preferences (such as dark mode or tutorial completion) are stored locally on your device using Android's internal Room database and preferences storage. You can clear this data at any time from within the app's History/Settings screen or through your device settings.

3. AI Help & Support Assistant (MRX Bot)

MRX Naamkaran includes an optional in-app Help & Support (MRX Bot) assistant to help answer app questions, suggest baby name ideas, and provide friendly conversational replies.

  • Messages you enter in Help & Support are used only to generate replies for your conversation.
  • Please do not share sensitive personal details such as passwords, OTPs, PINs, CVVs, financial numbers, or phone numbers in the chat.
  • If you choose Email Support, the app only opens a pre-filled draft in your device's email client—it never sends emails automatically without your confirmation.

4. 100% Free & Ad-Free App (No Ads, No Payments)

MRX Naamkaran is completely free and contains no advertisements, no ad tracking SDKs, no in-app purchases, no paid subscriptions, and no locked features. We never request or collect credit card details, UPI IDs, bank account numbers, or billing information.

5. Network Access & Permissions

The application requests only standard, non-invasive Android permissions required for core functionality:

  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to fetch baby name suggestions over secure HTTPS connections, power optional in-app Help & Support chat replies, and check if a newer app version is available.
  • Text-to-Speech (Voice Pronunciation): Uses your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech engine to pronounce baby names aloud. No audio is recorded.

We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, call logs, SMS, photos, or GPS location.

6. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with third-party advertisers or data brokers. Anonymous search filter parameters sent to our backend service (hosted on Cloudflare Workers) and optional Help & Support queries are processed strictly to deliver app functionality and are not used for ad targeting or profiling.

7. Children's & Family Privacy

MRX Naamkaran is a utility app designed for parents and families looking for baby names. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

8. Data Security & Retention

All communication between the app and external services takes place over encrypted HTTPS connections. Because your favorite names, family notes, and search history are saved locally on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing app storage permanently deletes all locally stored data.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app improvements or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
