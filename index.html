Summary for Users: MRX Naamkaran is built with a strict Privacy-First & Zero-Tracking philosophy. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal identity, device identifiers, contact lists, or baby details. All your saved favorite names remain 100% locally on your own phone.

1. Introduction

Welcome to MRX Naamkaran ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by Sonu Lal. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how our application operates and confirms our policy regarding the non-collection of your personal data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We believe in absolute transparency. MRX Naamkaran does NOT collect:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for your name, phone number, physical address, email address, age, or gender.
  • No Account Creation: No login, signup, or user authentication is required. You can use all features anonymously.
  • No Child or Family Identity: The app helps discover baby names, but never asks or records any child's identity or birth records.
  • No Device or Location Tracking: We do not access your GPS location, contact list, camera, microphone, SMS, or phone files.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Ad Trackers: We do not include tracking SDKs like Google AdMob, Facebook Pixel, Firebase Analytics, or third-party behavioral trackers.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app allows you to save favorite names, add personal notes, and create sibling pairs. All of this information is saved strictly inside your device's private local storage using an encrypted local SQLite/Room database.

  • This data is stored on your device only and never uploaded to any remote server or cloud.
  • If you clear the app storage or uninstall the application, your saved names and notes are permanently deleted with it.

4. AI Name Generation (Google Gemini API)

When you search or filter for baby names (e.g. by letter, rashi, religion, or modern vibes), the app securely requests name suggestions via Google Gemini AI API:

  • Only the non-personal search criteria (such as letter "A", gender "Boy", style "Modern") are sent to generate baby names.
  • No user identity, IP-tied personal identity, or device metadata is passed to the API.
  • Google processes requests in accordance with enterprise data protection standards.

5. Audio & Text-to-Speech (Speaker Feature)

MRX Naamkaran provides an audio pronunciation feature (🔊 Speaker button) so parents can listen to how names and meanings sound in Hindi and English.

  • This feature uses your Android device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech (TTS) Engine.
  • Speech synthesis happens locally on your smartphone.
  • No voice is recorded, and no audio files are created, sent, or uploaded anywhere.

6. App Permissions

MRX Naamkaran requires only minimal, necessary Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required strictly to communicate with the AI engine to generate baby names.
  • TTS_SERVICE: Required on modern Android devices (API 30+) to discover the phone's native speech engine for audio pronunciation.

We do not request or use any sensitive permissions (Camera, Location, Microphone, Contacts, or Storage).

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

While MRX Naamkaran is an application designed for parents and families searching for baby names, it is safe for all audiences. The application does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect app updates or regulatory requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
