3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The app lets you save your favorite names and keeps a history of your generated names. This information is stored only inside your device's local storage.

  • This data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers or any cloud.
  • If you clear the app storage or uninstall the app, your saved names and history are permanently deleted.

4. Name Generation and Rashi / Nakshatra Feature

When you generate baby names (for example by letter, rashi, religion or style), the app sends your selected search options to our secure backend service, which may use Google's Gemini AI to prepare the name suggestions.

  • Only the options you select (such as letter "A", gender "Boy", style "Modern") are used to generate names.
  • The "Janam Tarikh Se Shubh Akshar" feature uses the date and time you enter only to calculate the Rashi, Nakshatra and lucky letter. We do not use it to identify you, and we do not store it in any profile or account.
  • We do not send your name, phone number, email, contacts or any other personal details.
  • Like any internet service, the servers involved may technically receive standard connection information such as an IP address in order to respond to the request. We do not use it to identify you or to build any user profile.

5. Audio & Text-to-Speech (Speaker Feature)

The app provides a pronunciation feature (speaker button) so parents can hear how a name sounds.

  • This feature uses your Android device's built-in Text-to-Speech (TTS) engine.
  • Speech is generated on your phone. No voice is recorded and no audio is uploaded anywhere.

6. App Permissions

MRX Naamkaran requires only minimal Android permissions:

  • INTERNET: Required to connect to the name generation service.
  • TTS service query: Required on newer Android versions to find the phone's built-in speech engine for audio pronunciation.

We do not request sensitive permissions such as Camera, Location, Microphone, Contacts or Storage.

7. Children's Privacy

MRX Naamkaran is designed for parents and families looking for baby names. The app does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.
